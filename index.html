Forex Sentinel is a small always-on-top price widget for Windows and macOS: watch prices, note your own entry and see the difference, set price alerts. It is for watching and note-taking only and never places orders. Real prices come from your own MetaTrader 5 on the same computer; until it is connected the widget shows no prices and asks you to connect it. You sign in with an account granted by the Admin (contact: Telegram @thuongnv14 (https://t.me/thuongnv14)); each computer you use is recorded on that account, and to check the licence the app sends the computer name, a computer identifier and usage times to the Admin's server (see the Privacy Policy). Prices, notes, alerts and settings stay on your computer. Without internet the app keeps working for up to 7 days after its last check.

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" (common for newly released apps), choose More info → Run anyway.

macOS: universal build for Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11+. The first time you open an app downloaded outside the App Store, macOS asks you to confirm: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
